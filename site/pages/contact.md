## Report a problem with official information

Roadbeat shows data exactly as official sources publish it. If a road event, timetable or warning looks wrong, the source can correct it:

- **Road events and cameras:** the Department of Transport and Main Roads, on 13 19 40 or at qldtraffic.qld.gov.au.
- **Public transport:** Translink, on 13 12 30 or at translink.com.au.
- **Bushfires:** the Queensland Fire Department, at fire.qld.gov.au.
- **Brisbane gauges and traffic signals:** Brisbane City Council, on (07) 3403 8888.

In an emergency, call Triple Zero (000).

## About this project

Roadbeat is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the Queensland Government or any of the agencies above. Read more on the [About](/about/) page, and see how Roadbeat handles your information on the [Privacy](/privacy/) page.
