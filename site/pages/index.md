# Roadbeat: live Queensland roads and transport

Roadbeat is a free web app that puts Queensland's live road and public transport information on one map. It shows only what official sources publish, and it never estimates a time, a position or a delay.

## What you can do with Roadbeat

- See current road events across Queensland: crashes, hazards, flooding, congestion, roadworks and closures, from the QLDTraffic feed, refreshed every minute.
- Watch traffic and flood cameras.
- Follow TransLink buses, trains, trams and ferries in South East Queensland and Cairns, with next departures and delays, and see published timetables for Townsville, Toowoomba, Mackay and Translink's other regional networks.
- Read the full published timetable for any line or stop, for any day it covers.
- Plan a trip from the published timetable: direct services, or one change at the same station or stop.
- See Queensland Fire Department bushfire warnings, and Brisbane City Council stream heights, rainfall and traffic signal volumes.
- Get notified about events, routes and bushfire warnings that match your settings while the app is open.

## Where the data comes from

Roadbeat reads the public feeds of the Department of Transport and Main Roads (QLDTraffic), Translink, the Queensland Fire Department and Brisbane City Council, all under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. Base maps come from OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap contributors.

Roadbeat is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the Queensland Government. In an emergency, call Triple Zero (000).
